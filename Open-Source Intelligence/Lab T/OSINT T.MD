- Understand the fundamentals of Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)
- Learn the history and evolution of OSINT
- Explore common OSINT data sources
- Understand the role of OSINT in cybersecurity and digital investigations
- Prepare for upcoming hands-on OSINT laboratory exercises
