# Experiment 09 — Comprehensive Corporate OSINT Investigation

## Aim

To conduct a comprehensive **OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) investigation** of a well-known company and gather publicly available information about its history, key executives, financial data, partnerships, acquisitions, news mentions, and other relevant business information.

---

## Objectives

After completing this experiment, students will be able to:

1. Collect publicly available information about a company.
2. Investigate the company's history and major milestones.
3. Identify key executives and organizational information.
4. Collect and analyze financial information.
5. Identify partnerships and acquisitions.
6. Collect and verify company-related news.
7. Identify subsidiaries, products, services, and global presence.
8. Cross-check information using multiple reliable sources.
9. Prepare a structured Corporate OSINT Investigation Report.

---

## Theory

### What is Corporate OSINT?

**Corporate OSINT** means collecting and analyzing information about a company from publicly available sources.

For example, while investigating a company, we may collect:

> Company History → Founders → Executives → Products → Financial Data → Partnerships → Acquisitions → News → Subsidiaries → Technology → Industry Information

The information must be obtained from legal and publicly accessible sources.

# Ethical and Legal Guidelines

Students must:

- Use only publicly available information.
- Use information for legitimate educational/research purposes.
- Respect website terms and applicable laws.
- Avoid collecting passwords, credentials, or private information.
- Do not attempt unauthorized access.
- Do not impersonate employees.
- Do not publish sensitive personal information about executives.
- Record the source and date of important findings.
- Distinguish verified facts from allegations, opinions, and speculation.
- Do not attempt to exploit vulnerabilities discovered during OSINT research.

# Conclusion

Corporate OSINT helps investigators and researchers understand a company's publicly visible business profile by collecting information from multiple open sources and correlating the findings.

The experiment demonstrates how **search engines, corporate websites, financial filings, business databases, news sources, patent databases, and web archives** can be combined to create a structured and evidence-based company intelligence report.
